Fever and rash in children

  • 1Most rashes with fever are caused by viruses and get better on their own.
  • 2Do the glass test. If the spots do not fade under a glass, go to the emergency department now.
  • 3For fever, use paracetamol. Never give aspirin to children. Do not give ibuprofen for chickenpox.
  • 4Keep your child at home and away from pregnant women and newborn babies until your doctor says it is safe.
  • 5Check your child has had all their vaccines, including two doses of MMR.
